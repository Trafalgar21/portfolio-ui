01 About

I've built systems that process payments, systems that move millions of small events an hour, and systems that mostly just need to not fall over during a product launch. Each one taught me something different about where complexity actually belongs.

Lately I've been spending time on observability — not just logging that something broke, but building systems that tell you why before a person has to go digging.
